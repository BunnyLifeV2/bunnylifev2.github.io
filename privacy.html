Privacy Policy for "Bunny Life V2"

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Who uses this app

"Bunny Life V2" is a private tool used only by the owner of the channel "Bunny In Europe". It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

What the app does

  • It uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • It posts the owner's own videos on the owner's own Facebook Page, and it replies to comments left on that Page.

What data it uses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos, and permission to read the channel's basic details (to check that it uploads to the right channel).
  • Videos: the owner's video files, with their titles and descriptions.
  • Facebook Page comments: the text of comments on the owner's own Page and the commenter's first name, used only to reply to those comments.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own server. They are never published or shared.

Sharing

No data is sold. No data is shared with anyone, with one exception: to write captions and replies, the app sends the needed text and parts of the video (its sound and a few still images) to OpenAI, the AI service it uses for writing.

How to remove access

Access to a Google account can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
